LULLWATCH: FIGMA DESIGN BRIEF
=============================

TOTAL: 16 SCREENS (mobile), plus 1 design-system page.
Some screens also need a few small state variants. These are listed with
each screen and are not counted as extra screens.

Frame size: 390 x 844 (iPhone 14/15). Light mode for all 16.
Dark mode for screens 5 to 10 if time allows (see "Dark mode" below).

Deadline: the hackathon closes 14 Nov at 23:59 UTC, so time is very short.
Please send the Figma link by 13 Nov, end of day, in this order:
  FIRST (must have): 1, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, 14 and the design-system page.
  THEN, if time allows: the remaining screens.
The app has to be built from the designs and filmed before the deadline.


1. WHAT LULLWATCH IS
--------------------
Tagline: "Know when to go."

A phone app for a woman in labour. She times her contractions and the app
tells her when to start packing and when to leave for the hospital. It
counts her journey time and gives her a clock time, for example
"Leave by 4:36 PM".

The app also has a calm AI helper she can ask questions, by typing or by
voice. Pregnancy data is very private, so the AI runs on sealed, locked
hardware that nobody can look inside, not even us. Her phone checks a proof
of that and shows it to her as "Verified".

It is being entered in the NEAR Hackathon, in the "Privacy & Private AI"
track. The judges are investors and engineers, so the privacy proof has to
look trustworthy and clear, not hidden.


2. WHO USES IT, AND WHEN
------------------------
- A pregnant woman in early labour, usually at home. Often at night, often
  in pain, sometimes holding the phone in one hand while leaning on
  something.
- Her partner may be holding the phone for her.

So the design must:
- Be readable at a glance: one big message and one big button.
- Have very large tap targets. The main button is pressed during a
  contraction.
- Feel calm, warm and reassuring. Not clinical or hospital-like, and not a
  baby-pink cliché.
- Make the urgent "Go now" state impossible to miss, without being scary on
  the other screens.
- Show privacy as something she can see ("Verified", "Stays on this
  phone"), with the technical detail one tap away.


3. THE FOUR STAGES (the heart of the app)
-----------------------------------------
The home screen changes with her progress. Each stage needs its own colour
and feel:

  1. KEEP TIMING   : early on, relaxed. "There's time yet."
  2. START PACKING : getting closer. Get the bag ready.
  3. TIME TO LEAVE : leave now, or by a clock time. Text her partner and
                     call her midwife.
  4. GO NOW        : urgent. A warning sign was reported (for example
                     bleeding). Always overrides the other three.

Colours in the current build (please improve on them):
  calm green-grey, soft yellow, warm peach, alert red.


4. THE 16 SCREENS
-----------------

ONBOARDING (4 screens)

 1. Welcome
    - Logo, then the headline "Know when to go."
    - Text: "Time your contractions and Lullwatch tells you when to pack
      and when to leave, counting your journey time. Everything stays on
      this phone."
    - Main button: "Get started". Second button: "Try it with sample data".
    - A small privacy line, for example a lock icon with "Private by
      design".

 2. Setup, step 1 of 3: About you
    - "Is this your first baby?" Yes / No toggle.
    - "Hospital or birth centre" (example: St Mary's).
    - "Journey time there, in minutes" (example: 25).

 3. Setup, step 2 of 3: Who should know when it's time?
    - Optional. Text: "When it's time, Lullwatch writes the text and your
      own phone sends it. These numbers stay on this phone."
    - Partner's name (example: Sam).
    - Partner's phone.
    - Midwife or maternity unit phone.
    - A "Skip" option.

 4. Setup, step 3 of 3: When did your midwife say to come in?
    - Three numbers, shown as a sentence: "Contractions every [5] minutes,
      lasting [60] seconds, for [60] minutes."
    - Text: "Change these to match what you were told."
    - Button: "Start timing".

HOME / TIMER (6 screens, all the same layout in different states)
Every home screen has:
    - Top bar: logo, plus icons for Voice, Ask and Settings (or a bottom
      tab bar: your choice).
    - A STAGE CARD with:
        - headline (the stage name)
        - one-line reason
        - "Leave for St Mary's by 4:36 PM · in 1 h 12 min"
        - three small stats: Apart (5.2 min), Lasting (58 s), Rule met
          (Yes / 4:11 PM / –)
    - THE BIG BUTTON: "Contraction started" / "It stopped". This is the
      most important element in the app.
    - A small link: "Something feels wrong?" (opens screen 11).
    - A footer line: "Lullwatch helps you decide; it does not replace your
      midwife or doctor."

 5. Home: first use, nothing recorded yet
    - Big button sub-text: "Tap when one begins". The stats are empty.

 6. Home: contraction in progress
    - The big button is "on" and shows a live count: "It stopped ·
      Contraction: 42s". It should feel active, perhaps with a gentle pulse
      or ring.

 7. Home: KEEP TIMING
    - "Contractions are getting closer. Keep timing; there's time yet."
    - Show the sample-data banner here: "Sample data: a fictional labour,
      for trying the app. Start fresh".

 8. Home: START PACKING
    - "Your contractions are closing in on your midwife's rule. Get your
      bag ready."
    - Leave-by time shown.

 9. Home: TIME TO LEAVE
    - "It's time to leave for the hospital." "Leave for St Mary's now."
    - Two action buttons: "Text Sam" and "Call midwife".

10. Home: GO NOW (urgent)
    - "Warning sign: Bleeding from the vagina. Go to the hospital or call
      now."
    - "Head to St Mary's or call your midwife or emergency number now."
    - "Call midwife" is the dominant, urgent button. "Text Sam" is second.

FEATURES (6 screens)

11. Something feels wrong? (bottom sheet over home)
    - Six warning signs she can tap on and off:
        - Waters broke or leaking fluid
        - Bleeding from the vagina
        - Baby moving less than usual
        - Severe pain that doesn't stop between contractions
        - Bad headache, blurred vision or sudden swelling
        - Fever or feeling very unwell
    - Selecting any one sends home to GO NOW (screen 10).

12. Voice mode (hands-free)
    - She says "now" when a contraction starts and "stop" when it ends, or
      asks a question out loud.
    - Hint text: "Say 'now' when one starts and 'stop' when it ends, or
      ask a question."
    - A short privacy line, for example "Your voice is understood inside
      sealed hardware."
    - State variants (shown small on the same frame): Off · Listening… ·
      Hearing you… · Understanding… · Heard: "now" · Microphone not
      available.
    - This can be an overlay on home or its own screen. Your choice.

13. Ask Lullwatch (AI chat)
    - Title "Ask Lullwatch". Text: "Your questions are encrypted on this
      phone so only the AI inside sealed hardware can read them."
    - Empty state with three suggestion chips:
        - "How long until I need to leave?"
        - "What should be in my bag?"
        - "How do I breathe through this?"
    - Conversation state: her question, a short calm reply, and under each
      reply a small badge "✓ Reply verified" that opens out.
    - Loading state: "Thinking…".
    - Text box with a send button, and a mic button.

14. Privacy proof ("Verified" panel)
    - The screen that shows judges the privacy is real. It should feel like
      a trust certificate: clear, confident, beautiful.
    - Header: "Sealed hardware verified", plus "Encrypted to the sealed
      AI's key".
    - Section 1, "The AI" (6 checks, each with a tick):
        - The proof is fresh, made just now for this app
        - The AI runs on sealed hardware (detail: 3 sealed servers)
        - NVIDIA confirms the GPUs are genuine and locked
        - Intel confirms the CPU enclave is genuine and up to date
        - The key that signs replies lives inside that enclave
        - The software inside matches its published measurements
    - Section 2, "This reply" (3 checks):
        - The reply was signed inside the sealed hardware
        - Your words reached the AI unchanged
        - The reply reached you unchanged
    - Each check can open to show a small technical line in monospace.
    - State variants: "Checking with Intel…" (in progress), and FAILED
      ("✗ Verification failed: don't trust this", in red).

15. Contraction log
    - "Recorded (14)". A table with columns Started / Lasted / Apart, for
      example 4:02 PM · 58 s · 5.1 min.
    - Buttons: "Undo last" and "Clear".
    - A simple chart of gaps getting shorter over time would be a strong
      extra.
    - "Your contractions are stored only on this phone."
    - Variant: the confirm dialog "Delete every contraction recorded on this
      phone?" with Cancel and Delete.

16. Settings
    - The same fields as setup steps 1 to 3, on one scrolling page, with a
      "Save" button.
    - "Delete all my data" at the bottom.


5. DESIGN-SYSTEM PAGE (1 page)
------------------------------
- Colours: background, surface, text, muted text, lines, brand, and the
  four stage colours. Light and dark mode.
- Type scale: big headline, headline, body, small, and monospace for the
  proof details.
- Components: big timer button (off and on), stage card (4 stages),
  primary and secondary buttons, chip (off and on), input field, Yes/No
  toggle, verified badge (pass and fail), chat bubbles, bottom sheet,
  dialog.
- Logo and app icon. The current one is a placeholder.
- Icons: mic, lock or shield, chat, settings, phone, message, warning.


6. DARK MODE (if time allows)
-----------------------------
Labour often starts at night, so dark versions of screens 5 to 10 would be
valuable. Do these after the 16 light screens are finished.


7. RULES
--------
- Use the real wording above. It has been checked for safety and tone.
  Small rewording is fine, but don't change the meaning of any warning or
  health text.
- All names and numbers are fictional sample data (St Mary's, Sam, the
  times). Don't use real people's names.
- No ads, no account or login, no sign-up. There is none in the app.
- Keep the "does not replace your midwife or doctor" line visible on home.
- Use system fonts or Google Fonts only, so the build can match them.
- It is a web app shown at phone width. On a laptop it is the same
  phone-width column, centred.

- The design-system page.
- Any notes on animation, for example the timer button pulse and stage
