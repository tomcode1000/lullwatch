The hardest question in late pregnancy

“Is this it?”

01

Too early

Go in at the first strong contraction and you're often sent home, then back again hours later.

02

Too late

Wait too long and the journey itself becomes the risk, especially with a long drive or a second baby.

03

Too exposed

Pregnancy data is among the most private there is. In August 2025 a US jury found Meta liable for collecting Flo app users' period and pregnancy data.

How it works

Three taps from “is this it?” to the door.

01 · Time it

Tap, or just say “now”.

Tap the big button when a contraction starts and stops, or switch on voice and say “now” and “stop”. The log stays on your phone.

02 · Know when

“Leave by 8:36 AM.”

Fixed rules (5-1-1 for a first baby, unless your midwife said otherwise) watch the trend, forecast when you'll meet it, and subtract your journey time.

03 · Call and go

Know what to say.

“Call midwife” opens a short script for the call, written from your timer. One tap texts your partner where you're going.

Private by proof, not by promise

Nobody can read it. Not even us.

Lullwatch's AI runs inside sealed hardware (a TEE) on NEAR AI Cloud. Your questions are encrypted on your phone to a key that only that hardware holds, and your phone checks the hardware itself, with Intel and NVIDIA, before trusting a single reply.
